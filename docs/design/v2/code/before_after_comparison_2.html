NISAR SAR Limits

Synthetic Aperture Radar observations reflect satellite pass physics and surface conditions at image acquisition time:

1.

One pass every 12 days: Short flash floods occurring between satellite overpasses can be missed entirely.

2.

Flooded streets look brighter, not darker: Due to urban corner-reflector double-bounce, that street layer is less certain.

3.

Tide and waves near Wouri estuary masked out: Dynamic maritime water variations are excluded from inundation surface totals.
